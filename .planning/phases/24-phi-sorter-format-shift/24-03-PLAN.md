**HIPAA-correctness invariant (non-negotiable):** sorterData `category` semantics do not change. Old bucket model: drop on "PHI" bucket = claiming item IS PHI; drop on "NOT PHI" = safe. New stamp model is the same claim with job verbs: **REDACT = 'phi'** (it's PHI — redact it), **KEEP = 'not_phi'** (safe to keep). `handleStamp(kind)` maps `kind === 'redact' ? 'phi' : 'not_phi'` and feeds the EXISTING correctness check `item.category === bucket` untouched. Layout + keys preserve old muscle memory: KEEP/← on the left (was NOT PHI/←), REDACT/→ on the right (was PHI/→). Internal state may keep `'phi' | 'not_phi'` keys (e.g. trayCounts) — only presentation verbs change.

**Timer-expiry scoring (decided during planning, document in summary):** at 0:00 unstamped items simply never increment correctCount. `onComplete` fires with `totalCount = docSet.items.length` (FULL set) and the sacred formula `Math.round((correctCount / totalCount) * 12)` unchanged — so an expired shift scores proportionally to what was correctly stamped out of the whole pile. SorterDebrief (unchanged) will show "n / 10 correct" — accurate and honest. SorterCompletionOverlay band likewise computes off the full set; sub-60% shows the teal KEEP PRACTICING (encouraging, never a fail screen — SORTV2-14).
