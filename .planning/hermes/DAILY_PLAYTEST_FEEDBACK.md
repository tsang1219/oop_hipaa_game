# Daily Playtest Feedback

Add the newest entry directly below this introduction. Short, candid notes are more
useful than polished prose. Hermes should treat the newest entry as its strongest
priority signal, but it must still obey the nightly safety and verification rules.

Copy this template for each playtest:

```text
## YYYY-MM-DD

PLAYED: route, room, encounter, and approximate minutes
DELIGHT: what felt especially good or memorable
FRICTION: where I hesitated, got confused, or fought the controls
BORING MOMENT: where my attention dipped
BUG: what broke and how to reproduce it
MOST-WANTED CHANGE: the one improvement I want most
SHIP / REVISE / REJECT: verdict on the previous nightly branch, with one sentence why
```
